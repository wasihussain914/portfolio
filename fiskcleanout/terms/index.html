Terms of Service

Last updated: July 26, 2026

The short version: FiskCleanout is a free bulletin board where Fisk students buy, sell, and give away stuff to each other. We're the venue, not a party to any deal — you transact directly with the other student, in person. Don't list anything illegal, dangerous, or against Fisk policy, and be honest in your listings. If your item was imported from the Reuse, Fisk! GroupMe and you want it gone, we'll remove it — see section 4.

1. Who can use FiskCleanout

FiskCleanout is for people with a current Fisk University affiliation — students, and by extension anyone with a working @fisk.edu email address. Signup requires a @fisk.edu address and email verification; accounts registered any other way may be removed. If you graduate or otherwise lose your Fisk affiliation, you should wind down your active listings. You must be at least 18, or the age of majority where you live, to use the service.

2. What FiskCleanout is (and isn't)

We provide a venue: a place to post listings, browse them, and message other verified Fisk users. That's it. FiskCleanout is not a party to any transaction. We don't hold items, process payments, take a cut, ship anything, or guarantee that an item exists, matches its description, or that either side will show up. Every exchange happens directly between two students, in person, on or near campus — you are responsible for your own deals, including agreeing on price, payment method, and pickup.

The service is currently free — no listing fees, no transaction fees. If that ever changes, we'll update these terms and tell you before anything costs money.

3. Acceptable use

When you use FiskCleanout, you agree to:

  • Post accurate listings — real photos of the actual item, honest condition, real price.
  • Only list items you own and have the right to sell or give away.
  • Show up when you commit to a meetup, or message the other person to cancel.
  • Treat other users with basic respect (see the Community Guidelines).
  • Not scrape, spam, impersonate others, or use the platform for commercial reselling operations.
  • Not attempt to bypass the @fisk.edu gate or access other users' accounts or data.

4. Imported listings from Reuse, Fisk! — removal on request

To seed the marketplace, we imported listings that were publicly posted in the "Reuse, Fisk!" GroupMe group. Those original posters have not created FiskCleanout accounts, and we make that clear on each imported listing (they're marked with the poster's GroupMe name and a GroupMe contact panel, not an in-app account).

If one of those imported items is yours and you want it removed, we will take it down promptly — no questions asked. Email admin@fiskcleanout.com with the listing title (or link), or use the "Report" / removal link on the listing page. Details on Remove My Listing. The same goes for updates: if the item already sold in GroupMe, tell us and we'll mark it or pull it.

5. Prohibited items

Do not list, sell, or give away:

  • Weapons of any kind, including knives beyond ordinary kitchen use, and ammunition.
  • Alcohol, tobacco, vapes, drugs, or drug paraphernalia.
  • Recalled, expired, or hazardous products (including opened food, medication, and damaged lithium batteries).
  • Counterfeit or stolen goods, or items that infringe someone's IP (fake designer goods, pirated media, cracked software).
  • Live animals.
  • Anything whose sale or possession violates Tennessee law, federal law, or Fisk University policy (including the Student Handbook's rules on what's allowed in residence halls).

We remove prohibited listings when we find them or when they're reported, and repeat offenders lose their accounts.

You keep ownership of the photos and text you post. By posting, you give FiskCleanout a non-exclusive license to display that content on the platform so the marketplace can work. Don't post content you don't have rights to. We may remove any listing or message that violates these terms.
