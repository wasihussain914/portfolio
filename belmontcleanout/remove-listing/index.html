Remove My Listing

Last updated: July 26, 2026

The short version: if your item was imported from the Reuse, Belmont! GroupMe and you want it off BelmontCleanout, email admin@belmontcleanout.com and it'll be removed promptly. No account needed, no questions asked.

Why is my item on here?

To give the marketplace a running start, we imported listings that were publicly posted in the "Reuse, Belmont!" GroupMe group — items Belmont students were already offering to other Belmont students. Imported listings show the original poster's GroupMe name and point buyers back to GroupMe to reach them; nobody made an account for you and we don't have any of your data beyond what was in the public post (your GroupMe display name and the item details/photos).

How to get a listing removed

Any of these works:

  • Email us: admin@belmontcleanout.com — include the listing title or a link to it. That's all we need.
  • Report it on the site: open the listing and use the "Report" / "not your listing?" link — flag it as your item and we'll pull it.
  • Instagram: DM @belmontcleanout if that's easier.

Removals are processed promptly — typically within 24 hours, usually much faster. We'll also update a listing instead of removing it if you prefer (e.g. mark it sold, fix the price, or change the photo).

Rather claim it than remove it?

If you'd like to keep the listing but manage it yourself, sign up with your @belmont.edu email and mention the listing in your email to us — we'll transfer it to your account so you get in-app messages, can edit it, and can mark it sold.

Your own listings

If you created a listing yourself with your account, you don't need this page — open the listing while signed in and hit "Delete listing." For full account deletion, see the Privacy Policy.
